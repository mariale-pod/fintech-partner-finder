Methodology

Problems are framed as specific jobs to be done (e.g. "accept in-person payments" rather than just "payments"), grouped under 7 broader categories so browsing stays manageable: payments and acquiring, banking-as-a-service, lending and credit, cross-border money movement, wealth and investing, insurtech, and personal finance.

Company size is shown as a badge, not a filter default: nimble means privately held, generally founded within the last ~15 years, and either an estimated valuation under roughly $2 billion or a headcount under roughly 1,500 where valuation isn't public. Established means it exceeds one of those, is publicly listed, or is a widely recognized market leader in its category. This is directional judgment against available public data, not a precise financial threshold.

Partner scope: local (one primary market), regional (a handful of neighboring markets), global (broad multi-region presence). Selecting more than one problem shows only fintechs that address all of the selected problems together, not just any one of them.

Scope: this directory excludes the small set of companies synonymous with payments globally (Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Stripe). All other fintechs are eligible for inclusion, regardless of size or listing status, provided they solve a specific problem rather than serve as general-purpose payments infrastructure most readers already know.

Process: Research was gathered and organized with AI assistance to work at this scale. The problem taxonomy, tier judgments, and inclusion decisions are the author's own, checked against each entry's cited source.
